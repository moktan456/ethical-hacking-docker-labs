IT Onboarding & Account Policy  (HR / IT — INTERNAL)
Last reviewed: 2026

----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Account naming
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Every staff member is issued ONE corporate account used for email, the
HR file server and SSH access to the application server.

Username = first initial + surname, all lowercase.
  Example:  Rashid Khan  ->  rkhan

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Temporary passwords for new starters
----------------------------------------------------------------------
IT issues every new account a temporary password made of the MONTH the
staff member started followed by the YEAR:

      Month + Year         e.g.  March2026

(Months are written in full, capitalised: January ... December.)

Staff MUST change this within 7 days of their start date. IT runs a
monthly report of accounts still on the temporary password.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Reminder from the last audit
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The external auditor flagged that some accounts were STILL using their
issued temporary password months later. Please change yours.
