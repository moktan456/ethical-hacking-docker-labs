Re: Encrypted exports from the contracts system

Per IT policy, confidential exports (client contracts, renewals,
account-manager assignments) must be encrypted before leaving the
contracts system. Passphrase convention for these exports: the
responsible reviewing officer's title, followed by the export year,
no spaces, no punctuation.
